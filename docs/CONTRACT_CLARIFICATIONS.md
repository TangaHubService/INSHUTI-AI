# Contract amendment items before final acceptance

This is a drafting checklist for review by qualified Rwandan counsel, not legal advice.

The parties should sign one amendment that:

1. Identifies the Contractor consistently as NIYONKURU Bertin or TANGA HUB LTD and states the legal relationship between them.
2. Sets one total price. The contract currently uses both RWF 500,000 and RWF 600,000.
3. Replaces payment dates that predate the 16 July 2026 effective date and clarifies milestone certification.
4. Corrects article numbering and the payment table.
5. Replaces “end-to-end encryption” with the technically accurate acceptance requirement: TLS in transit, authenticated AES-256-GCM at application storage, provider-managed database/backup encryption, key management, and participant-only authorization. If genuine client-side E2EE remains required, make it a separately funded milestone with recovery/key-loss rules and independent cryptographic audit.
6. States that SMS, email, push, AI, mapping, hosting, domain, and third-party license fees require owner-provided accounts and ongoing payment.
7. Defines production acceptance as completion of the signed UAT, licensed clinical-content approval, independent penetration test/remediation, verified backup restoration, training records, deployment record, and source-code handover.
8. States approved RPO/RTO, availability SLA, support hours, warranty duration, data retention, youth consent/assent policy, safeguarding escalation, and incident-notification time.

Attach the final compliance matrix, UAT evidence, security report, clinical-review record, training record, deployment record, and acceptance certificate to the amendment.
