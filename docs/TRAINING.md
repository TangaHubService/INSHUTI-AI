# Training and knowledge-transfer plan

Run separate practical sessions for super administrators, content/admin staff, government users, healthcare professionals/CHWs, support engineers, and end-user advocates. Each session covers role boundaries, common workflows, privacy, safeguarding, emergencies, incident reporting, and hands-on exercises.

Evidence package: agenda, localized slides, trainer, attendance, environment/build, exercises, questions, competency check, feedback, recording where consented, follow-up actions, and signed completion record.
