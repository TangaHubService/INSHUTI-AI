# Clinical content approval record

Seed health articles are drafts and remain `NEEDS_REVIEW` by default. They must not be published merely to complete a software delivery milestone.

For each EN/RW/FR/SW article record: topic, version/commit, clinical reviewer name and license/authority, translator or language reviewer, evidence sources and dates, age appropriateness, emergency advice, cultural clarity, requested corrections, approval date, expiry/re-review date, and signatures.

The knowledge-base API prevents publication until title and body exist in all four languages. A licensed reviewer must still judge correctness. Production acceptance should include exported approval records and a sample audit showing the approving administrator.

The seed facility directory is also disabled by default and contains no phone numbers. Import only owner-verified facility coordinates, services, and contacts through the administration portal before locator UAT.
